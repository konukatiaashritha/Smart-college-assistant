Smart College Assistant

The Smart College Assistant is a web-based chatbot designed to help students quickly access college-related information without visiting multiple offices or searching through scattered documents.

What I can help with:

  • Admission procedures and eligibility requirements
  • Courses, departments, and syllabus information
  • Fee structures and payment methods
  • Examination timetables and results
  • Library, hostel, and transport facilities
  • Scholarship and student welfare information
  • Campus events and general college information

Answers are retrieved from a verified database of frequently asked questions. When I cannot find a relevant answer, I will let you know and suggest contacting the appropriate college department.

Note: Always verify important information such as deadlines, fees, and examination dates with official college sources.
